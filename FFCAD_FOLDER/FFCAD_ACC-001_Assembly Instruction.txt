PCB -001 Assembly Instruction
10/03/2026

The first-run PCBs suffered from several minor flaws that are addressed at assembly:

1) R5 ground is open.  Action: expose GND plane adjacent to open R5 pad and apply jumper.

2) D2 & D3 silkscreen is oriented backwards.  Action: install D2 and D3 opposite to the indicated silkscreen orientation.

3) Controller 4053-pin9 sense is reversed.  Action: place an inverter (74HC1G14xxx) at U10.

4) COS_HI_TRUE sense is inverted.  Action: Cut jumper ground connection (4 places at JU1 pad-1).
					Expose GND plane adjacent to JU1 pad-2 and place 10K to GND.
					Prior to JU1 install, route jumper from JU1 pad-1 to +5V (top side, U11-5).
					DNP R28 pullup resistor.

5) J1 interferes with RC-96 electrolytic capacitor.  Action: Install connector at J2 and run jumper for COS.
