RC-96 Front Panel Board Hardware repo (there is no software for this board).

CAD files are CircuitStudio (A.K.A. Altium lite).

Gerber & NCDrill files are in the "Default Configuration" folder.

An External 4x4 keypad (local DTMF entry) and a N.O. P.B. switch (reset) complete the assembly.
The assembly is designed for use with T-1-3/4 leaded LEDs that fit into 0.125" diameter holes
that are match-drilled into the front panel.  0.5" standoffs (0.375" may also be used) secure
the panel to match-drilled MTG holes (3).  The LEDs are placed, and then the board is secured
to the panel with 4-40 screws.  The LEDs are then guieded into the match-drilled holes and
soldered to complete the assembly.
